# Device sheet: 8-port desktop switch (`desktop_switch_8`)

**Family signature "desktop switches"** (guide, section 5): the same family as the 5-port switch (`desktop_switch_5.md`): a **flat slab**, light plastic, 1 px high; the **whole front taken by RJ45 ports** recessed 0.5 px, here **in two groups of 4**; the **LEDs on the top** along the front edge, one above each port, the power LED at the left end; vent dots on the top, rubber feet drawn underneath, the power input at the back. Told apart from the 5-port switch by its width and its two groups of ports.

Status: **chosen by Max on 2026-11-20** (preview `switches_scene`, `switches_fronts`), ports in two groups of 4. **Exported at lot 1d-1 on 2026-11-21** (`design/tools/export.py desktop_switch_8`, issue #18): block `DesktopSwitchBlock` (shape from the body of `PortLayouts.DESKTOP_SWITCH_8`), blockstate, item, loot table, pickaxe tag, language keys; checked by the capture scenario `device_switch_8`. Generated by `design/tools/desktop_switches.py` (never saved from Blockbench so far).

## Size

- Real: 158 x 102 x 27 mm (status E, `docs/network-equipment.md`, e.g. Netgear GS108).
- In game, x 2.4: 6.1 x 3.9 x 1.0 px, rounded to 6 x 4 x 1, **the width raised to 6.5 px** by the fit rule (guide, section 2): **6.5 x 4 x 1 px**. Envelope from 4.75, 0, 6 to 11.25, 1, 10.
- Java shape for lot 1d-1: `Block.box(4.75, 0, 6, 11.25, 1, 10)` for a front facing north, turned with the orientation.

## Do the 8 ports fit?

The front is 6.5 px, 26 texels. With the spacing of the guide (ports of 2 texels, 1 texel between two ports, 1 texel of margin on each side), 8 ports take 25 texels: they fit, with 1 texel to spare. **The spare texel goes between the two groups of 4** (2 texels there), as on many real 8-port switches: the front stays symmetric (1 texel of margin on each side) and the ports read as 4 + 4. Between two plugs of the same group, 0.25 px of free space (plugs as wide as their hole, `docs/cable-design.md`, section 4); between the groups, 0.5 px.

## Elements and texture

- `case` (4.75, 0, 6.5 to 11.25, 1, 10) behind a front panel 0.5 px thick split around the 8 ports (`front_top`, `front_bottom`, `front_middle_1` to `_9`), as on the box and the 5-port switch (`RecessedFront`).
- 64 x 64 sheet, 4 texels per px, colours of the palette only: top 0, 0 (26 x 16), bottom 32, 0, front 0, 20 (26 x 4), back 32, 20, sides 0, 28 and 20, 28 (16 x 4), hole ceiling 0, 36, floor 0, 40 (26 x 2), hole sides 32, 36 (2 x 2).
- Ports: RJ45, the single size (2 x 2 texels). Bottom in socket shadow tone with the contacts row at the bottom, contacts on the back row of the floor of each hole; ceiling and sides in socket colour.
- Top: power LED and the 8 port LEDs on row 1, 11 x 5 vent dots behind. Bottom: four rubber feet drawn in the corners.
