# Device sheet: laptop (`laptop`, `laptop_closed`)

**Family signature "computers"** (guide, section 5): **dark plastic**, like the PC tower; **flat when closed** (a lid with a highlight frame lying on the base), an **opened screen leaning back** when open; keyboard and touchpad on the deck, power LED on the front edge. Told apart from the desktop switch (light, flat, ports across its front) by its colour, its size and its screen.

Follows `docs/design-guide.md`. Status: chosen by Max on 2026-11-20, with a metal ring around the RJ45 port; exported to the assets with `design/tools/export.py`; check in game pending. Generated by `design/tools/laptop.py` (never saved from Blockbench so far): two block models sharing the texture `laptop.png`.

## Size

- Real: 360 x 240 x 20 mm closed (status M, `docs/network-equipment.md`).
- x 2.4: 13.8 x 9.2 x 0.8 px, rounded to **14 x 9 px**, base 1 px and lid 1 px (minimum thickness of the guide): **14 x 9 x 2 px closed**. Open, the screen (14 x 9 x 1 px) turns by 22.5 degrees around its front bottom edge and reaches y 9.32 and z 15.87, inside the block.
- FACING is the side where the user sits: for FACING north, the front edge of the keyboard faces north and the hinge is at the back (south).
- Java shapes (`LaptopBlock`, for FACING north, turned by `Shapes.rotateHorizontal`): closed `Block.box(1, 0, 3.5, 15, 2, 12.5)`; open, the base `Block.box(1, 0, 3.5, 15, 1, 12.5)` plus a box around the screen `Block.box(1, 1, 11.5, 15, 9.25, 16)` (within one texel of the turned screen).
- The keyboard is still 1 px high: the screen and keyboard gestures of lot 1c-1 (`LaptopPart.KEYBOARD_TOP`) keep working.
