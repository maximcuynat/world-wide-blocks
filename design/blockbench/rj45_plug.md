# Design sheet: RJ45 plug (`rj45_plug_<colour>`)

The plug drawn by the client in the port of each cable end (lot 1d-1, issue "Cable attached to its port, with its plug"; `docs/cable-design.md`, section 4). One model for every device, as a real RJ45 has a single size: the client turns it with the port (as the `y` of a blockstate: 0 for a port facing north, 90 east, 180 south, 270 west) and moves its mouth to the mouth of the port given by the port layout.

Status: **chosen by Max on 2026-11-20** (previews `plug_alone`, `plug_close`, `plug_scene`; `docs/images/cable-mock.png`). **Exported on 2026-11-20** (`design/tools/export.py rj45_plug_<colour>`): `models/block/rj45_plug_<colour>.json` and the shared `textures/block/rj45_plug.png`; names, paths and loading in `docs/cable-design.md`, section 4. No code of the game draws it yet: the client code comes with lot 1d-1. Generated by `design/tools/rj45_plug.py` (never saved from Blockbench so far): one project per cord colour, sharing the texture `rj45_plug.png`.

## Size and elements (port facing north, mouth at 8, 8, 8)

| Element | From | To | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `body` | 7.75, 7.75, 7.5 | 8.25, 8.25, 8.5 | Clear plastic, the section of the hole (2 x 2 texels, 0.5 px): 0.5 px in the hole (z 8 to 8.5), 0.5 px out (z 7.5 to 8) |
| `boot` | 7.75, 7.75, 6.75 | 8.25, 8.25, 7.5 | The long boot, 0.75 px, as wide as the body, in the colour of the cord |

- The cable (square, 0.25 px, the colour of the cord) is drawn by the code from the north end of the boot, z = 6.75, centred on the plug.
- Hidden faces left out: the tip of the body (always at the bottom of the hole) and the faces between the body and the boot.
- The plug and the bend of the cable take about 1.5 px out of the mouth; behind the tower and the laptop it stands out of the block (accepted, `docs/cable-design.md`, section 4).

## Texture

`rj45_plug.png`, 32 x 32, 4 texels per px: the body once, the boot once per cord colour (`design/palette.json`, key `cords`). **One model per colour, which only changes the UV of the boot** (Max, 2026-11-20); the client picks the model from the colour of the link.

| Area | Sheet (x, y, w, h) | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Body top, bottom | 0, 0 and 2, 0 (2 x 4) | Light plastic highlight tone (clear plastic). Top: the latch, a row in light plastic just out of the hole. Bottom: the gold contacts (contacts highlight tone) at the tip, inside the hole |
| Body sides | 4, 0 and 4, 2 (4 x 2) | Light plastic highlight tone, a shadow line (light plastic) on the lower edge out of the hole |
| Boot of colour n (blue, grey, yellow, green, red, orange, black, white) | x 16 x (n mod 2), y 6 + 6 x (n div 2): top, bottom (2 x 3), sides (3 x 2), end (2 x 2) | Base tone of the cord; a grip groove across the middle in the shadow tone; highlight on the top edge of the sides, either side of the groove; the end where the cable comes out in the shadow tone |

**Colour check:** the texture uses the cord colours, which the devices may not use. At lot 1d-1, `BlockResourcesTest` accepts the roles and the cord colours of `design/palette.json` for this texture and for the cord item icon only (Max, 2026-11-20; design guide, section 4), instead of an exception.

## Preview

    python3 design/tools/preview.py out.png --scene $(python3 design/tools/rj45_plug.py --scene)

draws the five devices with a plug in each of their ports (internet box LAN 1 to 4, 5-port and 8-port switches, laptop, tower), each with a cable stub to the floor, the colours in the order of the palette. `preview.py` turns each plug as the client will (`<id>@dx,dy,dz,angle`).
