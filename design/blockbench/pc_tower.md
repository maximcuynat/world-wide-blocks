# Device sheet: PC tower (`pc_tower`)

**Family signature "computers"** (guide, section 5): **dark plastic**, **tall and narrow**; a **raised vertical front panel** with an optical drive bay at the top, a metal power button standing out, the power and disk LEDs, two USB ports and dotted vents low; removable side panels with a vent field; at the back, the **metal plate**, power supply at the bottom. Told apart at a glance from the internet box (small, light, glass band) and from the desktop switch (flat slab).

Follows `docs/design-guide.md`. Status: front "classic" with the optical bay and RJ45 port low on an expansion card, chosen by Max on 2026-11-20 (preview `tower_variants`, `tower_backs`), exported to the assets with `design/tools/export.py`; build green and checked in game by Max on 2026-11-20 (four orientations, front, back, inventory, tower placed before the change, log). Generated by `design/tools/pc_tower.py --front classic --port low` (never saved from Blockbench so far).

**Port of 2 x 2 texels, chosen by Max on 2026-11-20** (previews `tower_2x2`, `rj45_tower_close`), exported the same day: the RJ45 port goes from 3 x 3 to 2 x 2 texels, the single RJ45 size of the guide, its bottom row kept, its contacts on the bottom row as on every RJ45 port; the link LED moves one texel closer. The Java shape and the model elements do not change, only the texture. Build and game tests green on 2026-11-20 (`BlockResourcesTest`, 36 game tests); not looked at in game yet.

## Size

- Real: 210 x 450 x 450 mm (status M, `docs/network-equipment.md`).
- x 2.4 gives 8.1 x 17.3 x 17.3 px, over the 15 px cap: reduced uniformly (1 px = 30 mm) to **7 x 15 x 15 px**, feet and power button included.
- Java shape: `PcTowerBlock.SHAPES`, `Block.boxZ(7.0, 0.0, 15.0, 0.5, 15.5)` (centred in x), turned by axis (`Shapes.rotateHorizontalAxis`); was `boxZ(8.0, 0.0, 15.0, 1.0, 15.0)`.
