# Device sheet: 5-port desktop switch (`desktop_switch_5`)

**Family signature** (guide, section 5): a **flat slab**, light plastic; the **whole front taken by the ports**; the **LEDs on the top**, along the front edge, one above each port. An internet box is the opposite: standing, with a glass band and large ports low on its front.

Status: **rough draft approved by Max on 2026-11-20**, kept as the starting point of the switch of lot 1d (issue "Desktop switch, 5 ports"). Not finished, not exported: the block, its blockstate, item, Java shape and language keys come with lot 1d. Generated by `design/tools/desktop_switch_5.py`.

## Size

- Real: ≈ 100 x 75 x 25 mm, provisional value, not verified (status M, `docs/network-equipment.md`; depth reduced from 100 mm on 2026-11-20).
- In game, x 2.4: 3.8 x 2.9 x 1.0 px, rounded to **4 x 3 x 1 px**. Envelope from 6, 0, 6.5 to 10, 1, 9.5.
- No plinth and no cover bevel: at 1 px high there is no room for them; highlight and shadow lines on the sides, rubber feet drawn under it.

## Elements and texture

- `case` (6, 0, 7 to 10, 1, 9.5) behind a front panel 0.5 px thick split around the 5 ports (`front_top`, `front_bottom`, `front_middle_1` to `_6`), as on the box (`RecessedFront`).
- 64 x 64 sheet, 4 texels per px. Ports 2 x 2 texels, pitch 3, holes 0.5 px deep: bottom in socket shadow tone with the contacts row at the bottom; floor, ceiling and sides as in the guide.
- Top: power LED and the 5 port LEDs on row 1 (the front is full, guide section 5), 6 x 3 vent dots behind.
