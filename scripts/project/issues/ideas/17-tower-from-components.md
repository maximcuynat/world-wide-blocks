Title: PC tower built from components
Labels: type: idea

Idea from Max, 2026-11-19 (interview before lot 1c): the laptop comes first, then the desktop computer, then components for more powerful machines. The tower would be crafted from its parts: case, motherboard, power supply, hard disk, network card, graphics card. Independent of the decision on issue #10: the terminal of the tower never depends on its components or its peripherals by default.

Each component should have an effect in the game, otherwise it is only a costlier recipe:

- network card: carries the identity of the interface and its MAC address (moving the card moves the MAC, as in reality); the Wi-Fi card of lot 2a becomes a card to add;
- hard disk: the size of the disk of the operating system, and what keeps the files when the tower is broken;
- processor and memory: the computing budget of the scripts of the operating system, as a rule the player can understand;
- power supply: its role comes with lot 1e (electricity);
- graphics card: little meaning for a network simulator, unless it conditions the graphical mode; to keep optional or to leave aside.

Points to settle when the idea becomes a lot: towers already placed in existing worlds receive a default set of components when the world loads; crafting recipes are planned at lot 9d (survival) and would come earlier for these parts.
