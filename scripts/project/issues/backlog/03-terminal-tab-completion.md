Title: Completion of commands with Tab in the terminal
Labels: type: feature, area: interface, priority: low

Pushed back from lot 1c-1 (decision of 2026-11-19, `docs/lot-plan.md`). Tab completes the name of a command, then its options and arguments (interface names, addresses of the ARP cache), as bash does; a second Tab lists the choices. The list comes from the registry of commands and from the real state of the machine, never from a fixed list.

To place when the terminal has enough commands for it to help, at the latest with the files of the operating system.
